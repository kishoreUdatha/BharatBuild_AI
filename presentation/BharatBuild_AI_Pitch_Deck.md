## SLIDE 3: Problem Statement

### The Problem

**Core Problem:** Indian engineering students and colleges are locked out of modern AI development tools and struggle with manual accreditation processes.

**Statistics (Market-backed):**
- 4.5 Million engineering students graduate annually in India
- 95% cannot access international AI tools (no intl. credit cards)
- 1,100+ colleges face NAAC accreditation every cycle - 80% do it manually
- Bolt.new/Cursor costs Rs.1,700/month - unaffordable for Indian students

**Who is Affected (ICP):**
- B.Tech/B.E. students (final year projects)
- CS/IT faculty (project management, OBE compliance)
- IQAC Coordinators & Principals (NAAC/NBA accreditation)
- College Management (compliance & rankings)

**Validation Timeline:**
- Building since January 2025
- Validated with 15+ colleges across Karnataka & Telangana
